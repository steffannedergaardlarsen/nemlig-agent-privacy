nemlig-agent

nemlig-agent is a private, personal tool used by a single household. It plans weekly family dinners and emails the meal plan and dinner suggestions to the household. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

  • The app uses the Gmail API with the gmail.send permission only. It can send email from the owner's own Gmail account. It cannot read, search, modify or delete any email.
  • The only emails it sends are the household's weekly meal plan and dinner suggestions, to recipients chosen by the owner.
  • The access token is stored locally in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's Gmail API.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
